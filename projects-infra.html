01

Production pipeline

Airflow Pipeline: Fix the Break, Then the System

Apache AirflowS3Cross-account data sharing (CADS)
Problem

A ~10-year-old daily pipeline that produces profitability reports. It ran almost every day and took 10.5 hours end to end.

One batch job pulled data through an API. It was designed for about 1.1 GB and grew to about 3 GB. At a ~3.3 GB limit the pull stopped working. The job also took 25 to 35 minutes.

What I did

Two tasks: make it work again, and make it faster.

  • Fix the break: an API cannot move data that large, so I switched to cross-account data sharing (CADS), S3 to S3. The job went from 25 to 35 minutes to 5 to 7 minutes.
  • Then the whole pipeline: the new pull changed what the pipeline needed. I compared hours used with hours alive, then tightened shutdown timing, right-sized instances and ran independent steps in parallel.

02

Data accuracy at scale

4M Cost-Center Migration

Apache AirflowPython rule engineDatabricksSHA-2 hashing
Problem

Commercial Banking (CB) merged into Corporate and Investment Banking (CIB). About 4 million cost centers, each with about 30 attributes, had to migrate.

Zero tolerance for data loss or duplication: one wrong cost center can cause a chain reaction. Checking every attribute is about 120 million comparisons. Too slow, and it wastes compute across environments.

What I built

I designed the migration system: Airflow-orchestrated batches, a 7-layer Python rule engine, Databricks for batch processing, then my validation layer. The validation layer is the part I am proudest of.

  • Level 1: one SHA-2 hash per record. 30 checks become 1.
  • Level 2: sum record hashes in batches of about 25,000, old and new, and compare totals.
  • Level 3: only for a batch that does not match, compare its record hashes, then open the one differing record.
Impact
120M → ~160

03

Team knowledge

Confluence RAG Chatbot

Confluence APIEmbeddingsOpenSearchAzure OpenAI (internal)
Problem

My team ran profitability reports. The knowledge was team-specific and spread across hundreds of Confluence pages.

Onboarding took about 2.5 to 3 months because new joiners kept asking the same people the same questions. I had felt this myself.

What I built

A RAG chatbot: "ChatGPT for our Confluence pages."

Pages are chunked in memory, since the text volume was small and no staging store was needed. Vectors, chunk text and source links go straight into OpenSearch.

Impact

Built about a year ago and still in use.

New joiners ask the bot and get answers with links to the source pages, instead of asking the same people again.
